CellSketch V2

Browser-based viewers for 3D cell analysis reports.

Stats Viewer

Overview, component volumes, presence across cells, and per-instance metric distributions. Load the joint report.parquet (or a per-cell report.csv) to compare multiple cells side by side.

report.parquet report.csv

3D Mesh Viewer

Interactive 3D scene and per-instance mesh gallery for a single cell. Colour meshes by entity or any metric. Load a per-cell report_meshes.csv generated with --with-mesh.

report_meshes.csv

Your data stays local — nothing is uploaded to any server.

CellSketch V2 is a derivate of Pixel Patrol and is developed by Helmholtz Imaging.
