What was found, and how big it is. Component Stats

A closer look at each structure. Box plots show the range of values across all instances — the box covers the middle 50%, the line is the median, and dots are individual instances. For multi-cell datasets, each box is one cell or group, so you can see whether they differ. Hover any plot for exact values.
